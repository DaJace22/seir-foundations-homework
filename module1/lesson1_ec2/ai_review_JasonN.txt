Q2: Why did "venv/bin/activate" give "Permission denied"?
AI's answer (my words): Excluded keyword 'source'. When it's excluded, command line executed the line as a program instead. Execute starts new shell, Sourcing runs inside current. Execute requires execute permission while Sourcing is read permission.
